Work / VerifyDesk

An email verifier that abstains instead of guessing

StatusBuilt and running, not launched
RoleDesign, build, benchmark
Period2026
StackPython services, PostgreSQL, SMTP probing behind a policy gateway

The problem

Every bulk sender pays for the same thing: a percentage of their list is dead, and sending to dead addresses damages the reputation of the domains that carry the good mail. Verification services exist to fix that, and most of them solve it by answering every address with confidence they do not have. A wrong invalid throws away a real customer; a wrong valid keeps the bounce.

What I decided, and why

A verifier that cannot answer should say so. Abstaining is a feature, and it must be free, or you have invented an incentive to guess.
  • Six verdicts, not nine. valid, invalid, catch_all, unknown, disposable, role. The three industry statuses I cannot derive from a probe (spamtrap, abuse, do-not-mail) are stated as unbuilt rather than faked from a guessed blocklist.
  • Charged only for definitive answers. unknown and catch_all cost the customer nothing, so there is no commercial reason to manufacture a verdict.
  • unknown is never cached. A stale "can't tell" outlives the condition that caused it. It is recomputed every time, because the alternative is a customer acting on last month's network problem.
  • A per-provider policy gateway. Microsoft and Yahoo accept every recipient at the edge and then tarpit, so probing them is a waste of an hour. The gateway holds that knowledge per provider instead of scattering timeouts through the engine.
  • Credits are append-only, and the schema enforces it. Balance comes from a ledger, not an updatable counter, and every query is scoped by organisation, so a job id alone never reads another tenant's row.

The measurement that matters

The dangerous error is calling a good address invalid. So I took 1,676 addresses from Leadmailer's own delivery history that had registered an open event, meaning mail actually arrived and the mailbox exists, and verified them through the production gateway.

Zero false negatives in 1,676 known-good addresses; the rule of three puts the upper bound at 0.18% at 95% confidence. Gmail, which carries most of a typical list, resolved 700 out of 700. Microsoft and Yahoo abstained on every address, and those are reported free.

The same benchmark states its own limits: it proves the verifier does not throw away good addresses, and it does not prove the opposite error, because that needs clean negative labels this dataset does not contain. Latency p50 was 559 ms, p90 21 s, max 77 s, driven by the two providers that tarpit.

Where it actually stands

The service runs end to end on a single box: signup, jobs, worker, results and filtered downloads. It is not launched: there is no product domain yet, and billing, identity checks and the launch email sequence are still open. I am listing it here because the engineering and the honesty of its results are the point, not because it is earning.
