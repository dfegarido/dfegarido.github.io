What it does

A marketplace that connects homeowners with tradespeople. Categories run from bathroom remodels and solar to gutters, plumbing, tree services and basement waterproofing, alongside a guide library and a cost-check flow for people who are still deciding.

What shaped it

A homeowner does not search for a marketplace. They search for a leaking basement.

So the service is the landing page. The application uses a dynamic route per service, which means adding a trade is content rather than a new template, and each page can speak to that job instead of the category. Two intents share the page: someone ready to hire, and someone pricing the work before they commit.

Outcome, stated honestly

The marketplace is live and taking enquiries. Lead volume and cost per lead sit in the client's ad account, so they are shown on a call rather than printed here. The repository has no automated test suite; review is by walking through the application and the schema.
