What it is

A directory and community platform for Muslim businesses and masjids in the United States, built from scratch as a freelance tech lead. Members join, businesses apply for a listing and carry a certification seal, and the site connects people to their local masjid. The distinguishing part is the money: thirty percent of every subscription is returned to the community, ten percent to the member's masjid and twenty percent to scholarships and interest-free loans.

The build decision that follows from the money

Because a subscription funds a masjid and a scholarship at the same time, the money is not a single balance. Three flows had to stay separate and auditable: a member subscribing, a business applying for a listing and being certified, and a masjid receiving its share. The platform keeps those as distinct paths — registration for members, a booking flow for businesses, and the split computed from the subscription rather than reconciled by hand at month end.

Why the model matters more than the software

A directory is a solved problem. A directory where the subscription pays for a scholarship fund changes what the product has to prove: every listing fee is also a donation, so the billing, the certification and the reporting all have to be defensible to a nonprofit board, not just to a customer.
