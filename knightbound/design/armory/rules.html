How it works

Set and rarity are two things

The set is the shape

Which beast a piece belongs to decides its name, silhouette and colour. Matching pieces count toward that set's bonus; the best set you wear applies, so two and two gives you the better pair.

2 · 3 · 4 pieces×1.08, ×1.16, ×1.25 on the set's stat. Flat, never scaling with the stage.
4 piecesadds the set's trait. The trait is the reason to finish a set rather than wear four better strays.
3 Dragon + 1 WolfDragon 3/4: ×1.16 damage. The stray Wolf boots count for nothing.
2 Wolf + 2 DragonTwo pairs: only one applies. A tie goes to the helm's set, so this is Wolf ×1.08 speed.

Rarity is the gem

How good a piece is shows as a gem (in the helm's brow and the item frame) and, on the helm, how closed it is. The set keeps the shape: a Legendary Wolfhead Hood is still a wolf, just in a mask.

  • Common ×1.0 roll · 1 stat line70% of normal drops
  • Rare ×1.5 roll · 2 stat lines22%
  • Epic ×2.2 roll · 3 stat lines7%
  • Legendary ×3.2 roll · 4 stat lines1%
  • Super Legendary ×4.5 roll · 5 stat linesbosses only, ~1 in 400
