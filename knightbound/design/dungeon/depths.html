Who lives down there

Four depths, twenty bosses

Each depth has its own four monsters (the waves are made of them) and five bosses, one per level. Nothing repeats across depths, and nothing from the meadow comes down here.

Depth 1 · Catacombs

Dungeon 1–5 · counts as stage 100–140

Bone and dust under the old city.

Depth 2 · Fungal Caverns

Dungeon 6–10 · counts as stage 150–190

Wet stone, glowing caps, things that crawl.

Depth 3 · Molten Forge

Dungeon 11–15 · counts as stage 200–240

The old smithies, still burning.

Depth 4 · Frozen Abyss

Dungeon 16–20 · counts as stage 250–290

Below the fire, the cold that never ends.

Past level 20 the twenty return Ascended: the same bosses in a darker palette, one more type each, and double the stats, so the map keeps going without new art every level.
