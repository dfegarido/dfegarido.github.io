Levels 1–70 as agreed; 71–100 continue the pattern and are a draft. Fast (the proposal) plays each strike like real lightning: every frame at 30 fps, only the last moment of the leader, the stroke and its two re-strokes, then a short fade, about 1.1 s instead of 2.3 s, and the bolts land close together. These are the game’s own strike on the game’s mushroom; the big bolt is that strike with its channel widened to 7 px once it lands. Today the game caps skills at level 20 and always drops five thin strikes (the Overview plays it).
