Knightbound Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · See also our Terms of Service

Knightbound is an idle pixel RPG for Android and iOS. This policy explains what the game collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or photos.

What we collect

Game progress

Your progress (stage, coins, upgrades, gear, skills and settings) is saved on your device. A copy is also stored online so it survives a reinstall or a new phone.

Your account

On first launch the game creates an anonymous account for you. It is a random id with no name or email attached. It stores your cloud save, your username, and your leaderboard records (best stage, deepest dungeon cleared, Souls, and the look of your helmet).

Your username

The game gives you a name such as Knight012. You can change it at any time in Camp › Profile. It is shown to other players on the leaderboard and in the Arena, so don't use your real name or anything personal.

The Arena

Once your run reaches stage 20, the game shares a snapshot of your hero's build with our server: your upgrade levels, hero level, worn gear, skills and Souls. Other players fight against that snapshot in the Arena and see it together with your username and Arena rating. We also keep your rating, your daily tickets and a short log of fights (which players fought, who won and how the rating changed). The snapshot contains no personal information, only game data.

Ads

Knightbound shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising id, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to show and measure them. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, using Google's consent message, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's settings. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Coin purchases are paid through Google Play (or the App Store on iOS). We never see your card or payment details. To confirm a purchase and deliver your coins, the store's receipt and an anonymous customer id are checked by RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy).

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the accounts, cloud saves, leaderboard and Arena (servers in Tokyo, Japan). supabase.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob serves the rewarded ads. policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat checks purchases.
  • Google Play handles payments and installs.

We do not sell your data. Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each player can read or change only their own save.

How long we keep it

Your account, cloud save, username, scores and Arena data are kept until you delete your account. Records of purchases may be kept by Google Play and RevenueCat where the law requires it.

Delete your Knightbound account

You can delete your Knightbound account and its data at any time. It can't be undone. The game on your device then starts over as a new player.

What is deleted

  • Your account id
  • Your cloud save (stage, coins, upgrades, gear, skills, settings)
  • Your username
  • Your leaderboard scores
  • Your Arena snapshot, rating and fight log

All of it is removed from our servers straight away when you delete in the game, or within 30 days of an emailed request.

What is kept

Records of coin purchases are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as tax and accounting law requires; they are not linked to your deleted account. Advertising data held by Google AdMob follows Google's own retention policy and can be cleared by resetting your advertising id.

In the game

Open Camp, tap the lantern crate (Settings), tap Delete account, then Delete forever. It happens at once.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email darwinfegarido@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Knightbound account” and your username (shown in Camp › Profile). We delete the account and everything listed above within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Children

Knightbound is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. You can delete everything yourself in the game, or email us to ask for any of these.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
