02

How an engagement runs

Three commitments, written down because they are the ones that get skipped.

Before: a written scope

One page. What exists, what changes, what deliberately does not, and the acceptance test for done. Agreed before any code is written.

During: weekly increments

Something you can open and use on a real environment every week, rather than a report describing progress towards one.

After: a handover that survives me

Documentation, credentials, and a runbook the next developer can follow without having to call.

03 · Contact

Tell me what is not working. I will tell you whether I am the right person.

A short description of the problem is enough to start. Email is the fastest route; LinkedIn works too if you would rather connect first. If it sits outside what I do, I will say so and point you somewhere better.

Useful in the first message: what the system does today, the stack it runs on, your rough budget range, and the date it has to be done by. With those four I can usually answer in one reply instead of three.
